# Dubai Travel Guide

## Transportation

Dubai has an extensive public transportation network including
Dubai Metro, buses, trams, taxis and marine transport.

The Dubai Metro connects major areas of the city and is useful
for travelers visiting popular tourist areas.

## Popular Areas

Dubai Marina is a major waterfront area with restaurants,
hotels, shops and attractions.

Palm Jumeirah is an artificial island known for hotels,
resorts and attractions.

Downtown Dubai is home to Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall and
the Dubai Fountain.

## Attractions

Major attractions include:

- Burj Khalifa
- Dubai Mall
- Dubai Marina
- Palm Jumeirah
- Dubai Aquarium & Underwater Zoo
- Dubai Miracle Garden
- Ski Dubai

## Food

Popular Emirati and Middle Eastern foods include:

- Machboos
- Al Harees
- Shawarma

## Best Time

November through March is generally considered a favorable
period for visiting Dubai because temperatures are more
comfortable than during the summer months.
